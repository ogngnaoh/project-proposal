# DesignVision

## What and why?

DesignVision is a mobile web app that helps people explore improvements to their living spaces. Users photograph a room and receive design suggestions tailored to their budget, preferred style, and existing furniture. The goal is to make small redesign decisions easier before spending money or moving furniture.

## For whom?

The intended users are students and renters furnishing small apartments or bedrooms. Initial testers would be recruited from classmates and friends who want to improve their own rooms.

## How?

A user uploads a room photo, selects a style and budget, and identifies furniture they want to keep. They can also provide dimensions and rental restrictions.

An AI model analyzes the photo and suggests changes to furniture placement, colors, lighting, and decoration. Users can request a generated concept image, refine their preferences, and save different ideas for comparison.

For example, a student could ask how to make a cramped bedroom feel more comfortable while keeping their desk and staying within a modest budget. Suggestions would explain their purpose, while generated images would serve as inspiration rather than exact measurements or guaranteed layouts.

## Scope

A team of 4–6 programmers would build DesignVision over one semester using Scrum. Short sprints would deliver usable features, starting with photo uploads and design suggestions, then adding concept images and saved designs. User feedback from sprint demos would guide backlog priorities. The initial scope covers one room at a time, excluding exact 3D reconstruction, structural renovation advice, and live shopping integrations.
